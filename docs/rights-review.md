# Rights review notes — Pellow River Postcards

Reviewed 2026-05-18 against the publishers' ledgers held in the reference
library.

## Findings

- The Pellow Printing Company ceased trading in 1941; corporate records were
  deposited with the county record office.
- Postcards published before 1930 are treated as public domain.
- Cards from Series 14 onwards were reissued by a successor firm; those carry a
  attribution requirement but no restriction on copying.
- No orphan works were identified in the collection.

Clearance for open access was signed off by the archive's rights panel on
2026-06-02.
